Wajah,

01. Teri hansi
Teri hasi kuch aisi hai, Jaise sukoon mil jaaye kisi dua mein, Bas ek baar tu muskura de, Toh dil kho jaaye usi ada mein.
02.Tera Mujhe Samajhna
Main sab kuch keh nahi paata, Par tu meri khamoshi bhi samajh leti hai, Mere chehre se mera haal padh leti hai, Bina pooche mera dil jaan leti hai, Aur yahi baat tujhe sabse alag banati hai.
03. Tera saath
Tera saath ho toh sab aasaan lagta hai, Har mushkil raasta bhi khaas lagta hai, Tu paas ho ya door, farq nahi padta, Bas tera hona hi dil ko sukoon deta hai, Kyuki tere saath mein hi mujhe apnapan milta hai.
04.Tera Khayal Rakhna
Tu poochti hai, “Khana khaya?” Toh usmein bhi tera pyaar nazar aata hai, Meri choti si pareshaani bhi tujhe fikr de jaati hai, Tu khud se zyada mera khayal rakhti hai, Aur isi liye tera care karna dil ko chhoo jaata hai.
05.Teri Choti-Choti Baatein
Teri woh choti-choti baatein yaad reh jaati hain, Teri awaaz dil mein kahin bas jaati hai, Kuch pal tere saath ke itne khaas hote hain, Ki baad mein wahi pal baar-baar yaad aate hain, Pata nahi kyun, teri har baat dil ke kareeb lagti hai.
06.Tera Mere Saath Rehna
Har waqt paas rehna zaroori nahi, Bas mushkil waqt mein saath rehna zaroori hai, Tu door hokar bhi mere paas si lagti hai, Teri maujoodgi ka ehsaas bana rehta hai, Aur bas isi liye tera hona mere liye bahut hai.
07.Tera Mujhe Behtar Banana
Tere aane se sirf meri zindagi nahi badli, Maine khud ko bhi thoda behtar paaya hai, Tu meri galtiyon par mujhe samjhati hai, Aur har baar kuch naya sikha jaati hai, Tere saath rehkar mujhe khud ka behtar version mila hai.
08.Bas… Tu Hai
Itni saari wajah likh di tujhe chahne ki, Phir bhi lagta hai kuch toh reh gaya hai, Shayad pyaar ki wajah koi ek hoti hi nahi, Bas tu hai… aur mera dil tere saath hai, Aur mere liye filhaal itna hi kaafi hai.
09.Tera Mujhe Chahna
Tera pyaar jatane ka tareeka alag hai, Kabhi lafzon mein, kabhi khamoshi mein hai, Tu bina kahe bhi bahut kuch keh jaati hai, Meri har choti si khushi mein khush ho jaati hai, Aur shayad isi liye tera chahna mujhe itna khaas lagta hai.
