Meri Muskan,

Agar tum ye khat padh rahi ho Muskan, toh shayad tumhe meri yaad aa rahi hogi,
shayad aankhon mein thodi nami, aur dil mein meri kami aa rahi hogi.
Toh suno, tum akeli nahi ho is ehsaas mein,
main bhi tumhe yaad kar raha hoon har saans mein.
Tumse door hoon, par tumse juda nahi,
kyunki mere dil mein tumse zyada koi aur basa nahi.

Muskan, teri yaad aaye toh chehra tera nazar aata hai,
khamosh raaton mein tera naam labon par aa jaata hai.
Kabhi teri hasi yaad aati hai, kabhi teri woh baatein,
kabhi mann karta hai bas tere paas baithun aur karta rahun tujhse mulaqaatein.
Pata nahi kitna pyaar hai tujhse, bas itna jaanta hoon,
tujhe jitni baar yaad karta hoon, utni baar aur chahta hoon.

Kabhi sochta hoon, kya tum bhi mujhe utna yaad karti hogi,
meri tarah meri tasveer ko dekh kar chupke se muskurati hogi?
Kya meri awaaz sunne ko tumhara bhi dil karta hai,
kya mera naam aate hi tumhara dil bhi thoda sa theher jaata hai?
Agar haan, toh Muskan, ek baat yaad rakhna,
jitna tum mujhe yaad karti ho, usse kahin zyada main tumhe yaad karta hoon.

Teri yaadon ka bhi ajeeb sa asar hota hai,
door hokar bhi dil ko tera hi intezaar hota hai.
Teri ek tasveer dekh loon toh din ban jaata hai,
tera ek message poori raat ka sukoon ban jaata hai.
Aur jab bahut zyada yaad aati ho tum,
toh dil bas itna kehta hai — kaash abhi mere saamne hoti tum.

Toh Muskan, jab bhi meri yaad aaye, udaas mat hona,
bas aankhein band karke mujhe apne paas mehsoos kar lena.
Main bhi kisi khamosh raat mein tumhe yaad karunga,
tumhari hasi, tumhari baatein, tumhara naam yaad karunga.
Aur jis din milenge, us din koi shikwa nahi rahega,
bas tujhe gale lagaunga… aur dil kahega — ab sab theek rahega.

Hamesha tumhara,
Navneet
