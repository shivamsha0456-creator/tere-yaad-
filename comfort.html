Agar Abhi Meri Yaad Aa Rahi Hai

Pehle ek saans lo. Ye ehsaas guzar jaayega, aur main bhi tumhe yaad kar raha hoon, shayad abhi is waqt.

saans andar… aur baahar…

1

Raat Mein Tumhari Yaad Aati Hai

Raat hoti hai toh tera khayal aata hai, Khamoshiyon mein tera naam sunai deta hai, Neend toh aa jaati hai aankhon mein, Par dil ko tera intezaar rehta hai, Pata nahi kyun, raat mein tu aur bhi kareeb lagti hai.

2

Jab Tumse Baat Karne Ka Mann Kare

Kabhi achanak tera message karne ka mann karta hai, Bas tera “hello” sunne ko dil tarasta hai, Baatein chahe kuch khaas na ho, Par tujhse baat karna hi khaas lagta hai, Kyuki teri awaaz mein ek alag sa sukoon hai.

3

Jab Akela Hota Hoon

Jab aas-paas koi nahi hota, Tab tera khayal mere paas hota hai, Khamoshi mein bhi tujhse baatein karta hoon, Yaadon mein tera haath thaam leta hoon, Aur akelapan bhi thoda kam ho jaata hai.

4

ab Tum Bahut Zyada Yaad Aati Ho

Kabhi-kabhi bas teri yaad nahi, tu chahiye hoti hai, Baatein nahi, bas tera saath chahiye hota hai, Dil karta hai sab chhod kar tujhe milne aa jaun, Tujhe saamne dekh kar bas gale lagaun, Aur keh doon—“bahut yaad aayi tum.”

tum bahut pyaari ho, achhe dinon mein bhi aur lambe dinon mein bhi  ·  Navneet
